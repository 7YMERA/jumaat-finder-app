    Good to know

    Tips for busy areas

    • Big mosques near malls fill up fast — the inner hall is often full ~10 min before azan. Aim to arrive early.
    • It doesn't have to be a big masjid — many surau in KL hold Jumaat too, and one may be right next to you. Ask once, then mark it ✅ so you know next week.
    • Leave a note on each mosque (parking, overflow area, how early it fills) — it's saved on your phone for next Friday.
    • Always confirm the time with the mosque; khutbah can start a few minutes after Zuhr.
